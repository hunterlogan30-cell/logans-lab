Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Logan's Lab is a personal health optimization app built for personal use. This policy describes how data is collected and used.

Data We Collect

Logan's Lab collects the following data that you voluntarily enter:

• Workout logs including exercise names, weights, and reps
• Body weight entries
• Daily schedule and checklist completions
• Journal entries, mood check-ins, and meditation logs
• Recovery metrics synced from WHOOP (with your explicit authorization)

How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely to display your personal health and fitness information within the app. Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

WHOOP Integration

If you choose to connect your WHOOP account, Logan's Lab will request read-only access to your recovery, sleep, strain, and cycle data. You can revoke this access at any time through your WHOOP account settings. We only access data you explicitly authorize.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. No one other than you has access to your personal data.

Third Party Services

• Supabase — database storage (privacy policy)
• Vercel — app hosting (privacy policy)
• WHOOP — fitness data (optional, with your authorization)

Your Rights

You can delete your data at any time by contacting the app owner or by deleting your records directly through the app.

Contact

This app is for personal use. For any questions, contact the developer directly.
